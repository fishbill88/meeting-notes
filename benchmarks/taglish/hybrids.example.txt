# Affixed hybrid forms appearing in the audio — one per line.
#
# These are the Taglish signature: a Tagalog affix on an English root, with the
# root keeping its English spelling. An English-trained engine drops the prefix;
# a Tagalog-trained one mis-segments the root. Neither is scored correctly by
# overall WER alone, which is why these get their own list.
#
# Used in Phase 7 to score hybrid-form accuracy separately. Scoring them via
# --terms works today, since the normaliser preserves word-internal hyphens and
# treats `nag-meeting` as one token.
